Our Community Promise

Why MarketNest Rejects Mainstream E-commerce Gimmicks

We don't use aggressive countdown timers or fake "Only 1 Left!" banners to panic you into buying. Artisan goods are meant to be chosen with reverence, knowing the exact workshop they originated from and how the raw earth was transformed.

🌱 Natural Materials Only

No plastics, polyester fills, or synthetic dyes. Only genuine clays, beeswax, and linen.

⚖️ 90% Direct Payout

Artisans retain full economic independence with guaranteed transparent payments.

📦 Plastic-Free Transit

Packed in biodegradable wood-wool and compostable plant-based kraft cartons.
