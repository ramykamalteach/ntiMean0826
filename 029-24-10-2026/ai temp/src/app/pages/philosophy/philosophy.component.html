Our Ethical & Visual Manifesto

The MarketNest Creed

We believe that the way an online marketplace is designed should reflect the integrity of the objects sold within it. Here is why we do things differently.

01

No Drop Shadows: The Honesty of Borders

Corporate retail uses heavy dropshadows to manufacture artificial depth on a digital screen. At MarketNest, we follow a flat, border-driven design philosophy. Depth is achieved naturally through warm cream layering (#FFFBF5) and crisp stone borders (#E7E5E4 and #D6D3D1). The visual simplicity allows the tactile authenticity of raw stoneware, oak grain, and linen weave to command the viewer’s full attention.

02

Zero Manipulative Pressure Tactics

You will never find flashing countdown timers, fake stock alerts ("Only 1 left! Hurry!"), or high-pressure popups on MarketNest. True craft is made for patient living. An artisan teapot or leather satchel is an investment intended to serve your household for decades. We give buyers space, quiet, and comprehensive provenance so decisions are rooted in admiration, not anxiety.

03

90% Direct Artisan Revenue

Mainstream department stores and wholesale distributors mark products up 400% while paying creators a meager fraction of retail value. On MarketNest, 90 cents of every dollar spent flows directly into the artisan’s studio account. This ensures master craftspeople can afford sustainable wild materials, fair studio wages, and the freedom to experiment.

04

Uncompromising Material Provenance

Every material on our platform is transparently sourced: storm-felled Swedish timber, Oaxaca river silt, unbleached Provencal beeswax, and vegetable-tanned hides. All goods are packed in 100% compostable wood-wool and unbleached kraft cardboard, with zero synthetic bubble wrap or single-use plastics.
